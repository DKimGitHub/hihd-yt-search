윤홍식의 견성요결-나에게서 찾으라(Seek within Me)

2013-01-29 · 6:40 · YouTube에서 보기

0:00

Seek within Me. I hope you just do well, intensely “Don’t know.” and “It’s alright.” among other things at the Hongik Academy. Wherever you go around the world, if you practice just this... After all, people travel all over the world to seek for the true-self.

0:13

The way is not hard. We already possess it and what we want to know is just ourselves Simply say, “I don’t know.” and “It’s alright.” and you can’t help but encounter the true-self. Once you meet it, do so deeply.

0:27

As I say often, even for Venerable Kyung Hur, a known genius, once he had had a glimpse of the true-self during his meditation using pithy phrases. He sat down for a whole year just to look at it at Cheon Jang cottage even without cleaning himself.

0:43

If we go on living the life same as before after meeting the true-self, nothing will be changed. Please remember that spending a year to meet with the true-self deeply is not a waste of time at all, once you feel it even a tiny degree.

0:57

At Buddhist temples, there is this beautiful saying. “Do practice around the clock as if you were not born to this present life.” Would it not be good to do so just for a day, if not a whole life?

1:08

If you meet with the true-self for a day deeply, The experience of meeting with the true-self frequently becomes a power that buttresses you. You are to know how the true-self works and affects within you bit by bit.

1:22

Please try that. Doing just that will be sufficient. Though, the academy advocates the Four Beginnings or the Conscience first and foremost. Honestly, I am quite confident that we are the second to none when it comes to help others obtain ‘the seeing the true-self.’ Quite quickly and easily.

1:38

If we were a business venture, simply selling only this 'product' would probably suffice. However, why we do this is to live conscientiously. Thus, we emphasize the conscience. Being fast is not particularly important.

1:55

We, better than any others, can tell you that seeing the true-self is easy and it is a culture that anyone could be blessed with. At the academy, simply say, “I don’t know.” There are various ways of doing “I don’t know.” You may say, “I don’t know.” seriously or you may briefly imagine,“I really don’t know.”

2:15

When your heart is heavy or if your ego feels uncomfortable, you can’t carry out meditation well. You ought to adjust to the condition of your ego. When the ego finds meditation hard going, say, “oh well, I don’t care.” and stay still. Say, “I don’t know.” and relax.

2:32

After all, saying “I don’t know.” is letting the outer world, my adherence or preconception go. The peace will find you if your ego relaxes just a bit slightly. Calm your ego tenderly. When your ego grabs hard and not let go of, soothe it. And you take care of it.

2:55

“Hold on, let me drop everything, say, just for five minutes.” or “I’ll think about it five minutes later.” or “l’ll take a break for five minutes.” “Oh well, I don’t know.” or “Be my guest.” all these methods will do. Indeed, we are to return to the pure state automatically as we relax the grip of the hands.

3:19

Also, you can reach there by formally doing, “I don’t know.” or by doing it precisely, or by imagining briefly, “I really don’t know.” By the way, what is funny is that we usually seek for the true-self not within us.

3:34

The heart gets excited when we see some light or something during the meditation. Whatever you name it, that is actually one of the five senses. That is an object that appears in my mind, not ‘I’. What I am telling you is that all the clues for finding the true-self are within the word it-self.

3:51

It is inconceivable that you don’t know where the true-self is when you say, “I want to find true-self per se.” If you understand it, the true-self is ‘I’. You must find it within the sense of ‘I’. You should not digress even a bit from the sense of ‘I’.

4:04

Right now, you have the sense of ‘I’. It is the natural feeling, when you say,‘I’. Say,‘I’ in your heart. That’s the true-self. The place that resonates with ‘I’. That's the place where you can find the true-self. So, don't look for any other places, like wonderful scenary, beatiful church or temple

4:24

or whatever located outside of ‘I’ including a wish to see God. All these are not ‘the place’. Seek within you if you want to meet the true-self. The true-self is the purest foundation within us.

4:34

Even the sense of ‘I’ gives impure feelings, like "I'm so smart." or any other identification of "I" contaminated with various feelings. Seek for the ‘purest I’. Thus, saying often, “I don’t know.” is a good thing. It clears all.

4:51

“I think I am important.” “I don’t know.” If you keep saying, “I don’t know, I don’t know, and I don’t know.” the time will come when you can no longer say, “I don’t know.” When will that be? That’s when you simply exist. The time in which ‘I’ exists, when you can’t say, “I don’t know.” any more.

5:05

You can’t possibly deny the existence of ‘I’. The state where ‘I’ exists, however, not a single thing appears in the mind and everything else is ‘ignored’. Even for a second, that is when the true-self appears.

5:16

When you meet with ‘I’ again and again, the second becomes two seconds and three seconds so on and so forth, and eventually the tables will be turned. After all, you will reach the state where the true-self is continuously present and the ego comes and goes.

5:29

The true-self never leaves me. What arises and disappears are thoughts, not the true-self. It is the ground on which thoughts, emotions and senses appear. I think, cry or laugh as ‘I’ exist. Ordinarily, we forget ‘I’ and only thought, crying or laughing occupies the center of our mind.

5:48

This shift of the point of view must occur. This shift of the point of view occurs. And this is truly the practical way of awakening. When applied, anybody can not but see the true-self Because we all are already seeing the true-self... Because, there is no one who doesn’t have ‘I’.

6:04

Knowing that ‘I-Am’ is enlightenment. That is ‘I’ always exists. ‘I’ who cries, laughs, rejoices and grieves, exists. That ‘I’ is not the laugh, the sadness nor the joy, but simply the being. When you are able to say these with solid sense, you are a Master.

6:25

That is to say, these are the words only the enlightened one can say. And yet, these are not difficult ones at all. Because, you folks are a semi-Master. There is no one who does not sense ‘I’. Even though every one senses it, folks go on living not knowing that is the true-self.
